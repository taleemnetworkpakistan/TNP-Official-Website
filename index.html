Who We Are

Our Vision & Mission

TNP is a voluntary, nation-building project under the Nazriya Pakistan Council (Trust) at Aiwan-i-Quaid, Islamabad — dedicated to universal literacy for Pakistani boys and girls.

📖

Vision

Knowledge is bliss. It makes a person complete. TNP strives to bring literacy to every Pakistani child — boys and girls alike — so they may know their full potential.

🎯

Mission

TNP envisages making a valuable contribution to Pakistan's literacy drive. Through philanthropic support, we enrol out-of-school children and provide quality education through Taleem o Tarbiyat & Tazkiyya.

The Concept

How TNP Works

TNP's first project establishes two dedicated Non-formal Primary Classes with an Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) on existing school premises, starting in Meharbadi, Islamabad Capital Territory.

A 2023 JICA survey confirmed that large numbers of primary-age children remain out of school across almost all union councils of ICT.

Non-Formal Primary Classes

Two model ALP classes held on running school premises — removing barriers for out-of-school children.

JICA Partnership

Planned as a collaborative arrangement with the Japan International Cooperation Agency.

No Bureaucratic Hurdles

TNP keeps its model simple — empowering philanthropists to act directly.

Short-Term, Scalable Projects

Each cycle is designed as a short-term project that can be replicated across Pakistan.

National Context

Pakistan's Education Policy

Pakistan's Constitution guarantees education as a fundamental right. TNP's work directly supports national policy goals.

Single National Curriculum (SNC) to ensure uniform standards across all schools.

100% enrollment at primary and secondary levels — eliminating gender and regional disparities.

Expansion of technical and vocational training programs linked to employment.

Promotion of girls' education and integration of digital learning in schools.

Strengthening teacher training and modernizing school infrastructure.

Alarming Reality

The Stakes Are High

When children remain outside the school system and grow up illiterate, the consequences ripple through all of society. Without education, young people become vulnerable to exploitation, extremism, and poverty.

The Taleem Network Pakistan exists to break this cycle. With every citizen's cooperation, we can banish illiteracy and mould future generations into educated, enlightened, and prosperous members of the global community.

It is a gigantic task — but not Mission Impossible.

⚠ Without education, children risk...

  • Falling prey to anti-social elements and extremism
  • Drug addiction and criminal pathways
  • Permanent poverty and menial labour
  • Exclusion from civic and national life
  • A bleak and dangerous future for society
Get Involved

Action Plan

TNP's action plan is designed for simplicity. No bureaucracy. No red tape. Just ordinary Pakistanis choosing to change a child's fate.

1

Identify a child in your area who is of primary school age but not attending school.

2

Choose a school — any affordable school, from Rs. 50/month to Rs. 500/month.

3

Sponsor their education — cover fees, stationery, or uniforms.

4

Watch them flourish — the day that child excels will be your greatest moment of pride.

Hold the Hand of One Child

This is our fervent appeal to the well-to-do brothers and sisters of this beloved country: come forward and get one — or more — child admitted to a school. The primary aim is to bring deserving children within the boundary walls of a school.
